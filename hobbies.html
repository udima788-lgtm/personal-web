MY FAVOURITE ACTIVITY

Running

Running is one of my favourite ways to stay fit and clear my head. I enjoy the feeling of progress, setting a distance goal and gradually getting better.

It doesn't matter if the run is short or long. The most important thing is getting outside, being consistent and enjoying the process.
